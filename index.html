Why trust these numbers

Calories start from the Mifflin-St Jeor resting energy equation published by Mifflin, St Jeor et al., American Journal of Clinical Nutrition 1990;51(2):241–247, multiplied by one of five standard activity factors (1.2 to 1.9). The protein range of 1.6–2.2 g per kg comes from the break-point analysis in Morton et al., British Journal of Sports Medicine 2018, which put the plateau in training-induced gains in fat-free mass at 1.62 g/kg/day with a 95% confidence interval of 1.03 to 2.20. The ISSN position stand on protein and exercise (Jäger et al., 2017) is the cross-check. Food protein figures come from USDA FoodData Central. Sources were last checked on 19 September 2026. The site is maintained by the Body Recomp Calc editorial team; the goal adjustments and the 25% fat share are starting-point conventions, not clinical figures. Found a mistake? Tell us.
